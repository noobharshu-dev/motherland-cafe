# Motherland Cafe Website

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### Overview
Motherland Cafe is a premium cafe in Kolkata focused on artisanal coffee, healthy food, gluten-free options, handcrafted beverages, and a calm work-friendly atmosphere.

### Business Goals
- Increase walk-in customers
- Improve online presence
- Showcase ambience and menu
- Enable reservations
- Improve SEO visibility

### Target Audience
- Young professionals
- Students
- Remote workers
- Coffee lovers
- Health-conscious customers
- Tourists

### Features
- Homepage
- About Section
- Menu Section
- Gallery
- Reviews
- Contact Page
- Reservation Form

### Success Metrics
- Increased reservations
- Increased website traffic
- Higher menu engagement
- Better local search rankings
